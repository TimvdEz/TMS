✈ YOUR NEXT DESTINATION

A PROJECT THAT CROSSES BORDERS.

ProjectINc brings students together across countries to design for people who are too often overlooked by digital systems. This mini-brief is only the first stop.

One team, different instincts.

Different countries. Different design habits. One real client brief. In ProjectINc, mixed teams research, question assumptions, sketch, prototype and test ideas for a more inclusive digital world.

What you'll experience

  • International and multidisciplinary teamwork
  • Real challenges around inclusion and accessibility
  • Design thinking, playful experimentation and prototyping
  • Online collaboration followed by an in-person week in Groningen
  • New perspectives, connections and portfolio experience
5 ECTSStudy credits

Before you board

Do I need to be a design expert?

No. Curiosity, collaboration and a willingness to learn matter. Eligibility depends on your university and study programme.

Is this the actual BIP application?

Registration is handled by your participating university. Contact your coordinator for the official process and dates.

What will I work on?

Teams tackle real inclusion challenges using a design-thinking process: understand, observe, define, ideate, prototype and test.

Where do I get dates and costs?

Ask your university's ProjectINc coordinator for the current edition's dates, selection criteria, travel arrangements and any funding information.
